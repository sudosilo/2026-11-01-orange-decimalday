Decimal Day Clock

One day is 86,400 SI seconds. Eight clean base 100 blocks, then a pocket.

0.00.00
Day count
0.00000
Block: 10,000 ticks. Minute: 100 ticks. Tick: one SI second, the same length as today. The day starts at your local midnight. Blocks 0 through 7 fill 80,000 ticks. The last 6,400 ticks of the day are the pocket, counted as 64 minutes of 100 ticks. The pocket is the special time. It starts at 22:13:20 on the old clock.
